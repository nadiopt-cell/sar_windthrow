about=Plugin for rapid windthrow mapping from SAR data: STAC search and COG download on Microsoft Planetary Computer (Sentinel-1 GRD or RTC), preprocessing (dB conversion, Lee speckle filter, land/water mask) and three validated detection modes (v1.0): (1) C-band amplitude WI = dVV + dVH with adaptive threshold after Ruettschi et al. 2019, background normalization (v0.8) and an optional forest mask — v1.1 adds the GFW Hansen GFC reconstruction on the year preceding the event (retrospective mask of record) next to ESA WorldCover and user files; (2) L-band backscatter decline on ALOS PALSAR after Tanase et al. 2018 (LDI = dHH + dHV, inverted sign); (3) interferometric coherence difference-in-differences over ASF HyP3 products (dcoh = coh_control − coh_prepost) — v1.2 accepts both full-frame INSAR-GAMMA (80 m) and Burst InSAR pairs (INSAR_ISCE_BURST, 20/40/80 m) with same-burst DiD validation, and fixes the product water-mask convention (1 = land, 0 = water per the ASF guides). All modes produce a float32 index raster, a byte mask and a GeoPackage with per-object area in hectares.
